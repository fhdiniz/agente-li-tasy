# Account Receivable

Allows for the creation and searching of accounts receivable and settlements in Tasy by a third party.

<br>

- Available endpoints
   - Search accounts receivable
   - Search accounts receivable to expire
   - Search expired accounts receivable
   - Search taxes of accounts receivable
   - Search classifications (apportionments by cost center and ledger account) of accounts receivable
   - Search settlements of accounts receivable
   - Perform settlements of accounts payable
   - Update settlements external code and integration date
