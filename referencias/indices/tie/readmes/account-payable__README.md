# Account Payable

Allows for the searching and creation of accounts payable and their classifications and the performing of settlements in Tasy by a third party.

<br>

- Available endpoints
   - Search accounts payable
   - Search payable taxes
   - Search the classification (apportionments by cost center and ledger account) of accounts payable
   - Perform settlements of accounts payable
   - Create accounts payable and classifications (apportionments by cost center and ledger account)
