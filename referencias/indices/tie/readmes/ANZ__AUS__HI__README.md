### Health care Identifier

Integration with HI Service.  
Uses SOAP Calls with https4 Protocol for the communication between TASY and HI service

A healthcare identifier is a unique number that has been be assigned to individuals, and to healthcare providers and organisations that provide health services. The identifiers are assigned and administered through the HI Service  which   was established to undertake this task

Purpose: Create/Update patient health record(ConsumerSearchIHI) and physician  record (ProviderSearchIHI) allows a trusted party to search for the details of a Healthcare Individual within the HI service

Communication will start from Tasy and then soap webservice call to HI service which inturn HI 
will send Success(IHI number) or Failure to Tasy

The following are the messages which we will be part of the integration between TASY and HI Service

1.Request elements for the Healthcare Individual(Consumer Search IHI)
2.Request elements for the Healthcare Provider(Provider Search HPI-I)
